What Travis asked for

  • The base layer first: one movement's complete evidence chain as one exportable artifact, in minutes, with no manual stitching (Q10.2), so every dollar moves under an enforced policy with evidence an examiner, auditor or LP accepts (Q10.6).
  • Add-ons later: zero-knowledge selective disclosure and the agent layer.
  • An architecture that travels: built with Salt and Parsera in mind, so fund number two on the same stack is mostly configuration.

Acceptance criteria for the pilot test

  • All eight negative-path scenarios refuse, visibly, with the right reason code, and none can be overridden.
  • One approved movement produces a complete Movement Evidence Chain export in minutes, with no manual step.
  • Tampering with any recorded fact is detected by chain verification.
  • The Salt transaction id is bound to the MRID at submission, and every Salt state change is recorded.
  • Synthetic data only; no key material, credentials or investor data reach Q².
